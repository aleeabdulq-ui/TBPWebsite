Who We Are

The Building Practice Ltd.

The Building Practice Ltd. (TBP) is an architectural firm dedicated to shaping spaces that inspire, endure, and connect. Since its founding in October 2013, TBP has evolved into a trusted name in the Nigerian built environment, delivering thoughtful, sustainable, and contextually relevant designs across residential, commercial, hospitality, educational, retail, industrial and institutional sectors.

From concept development to construction management, every TBP project reflects a deep commitment to functionality, beauty, and cultural resonance. We approach each design challenge as an opportunity to tell a story - one that reflects our client's purpose, the site's character, and our belief in architecture as a transformative force.
