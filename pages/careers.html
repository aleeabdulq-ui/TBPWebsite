Frequently Asked

Contact HR Team About TBP Services
Do I need a portfolio to apply?

Yes, a portfolio is essential for all design roles. It should showcase your best work, including academic projects, personal work, or professional work you're permitted to share. We're looking for demonstration of design thinking, technical skills, and creativity.

What software skills are required?

Proficiency in AutoCAD is essential. Knowledge of Revit, Rhino, SketchUp, or other BIM/3D software is a plus. We're also happy to train promising candidates in tools they haven't yet mastered.

Do you offer internships?

Yes! We offer internship programs for architecture students and recent graduates throughout the year. Our internships provide real project experience, mentorship, and potential full-time opportunities for top performers.

Can international applicants apply?

Yes, we welcome international applications. However, candidates must have or be eligible to obtain the appropriate work permit. We can provide sponsorship for exceptional candidates.
