Adebiyi Gbolahan

A team-oriented architect with a strong foundation from Obafemi Awolowo University and practical experience in property development, bringing a unique understanding of cost, taste, and functionality to architectural design.

Adebiyi Gbolahan is a graduate of Obafemi Awolowo University, Ilé-Ifè̀ where he obtained a BSc and MSc in 2019 and 2022 respectively. He worked previously in the property development sector where he was exposed to the dynamics of cost, taste and functionality and how they relate to architecture.

As one who believes in teamwork, he is very friendly, eager to learn and contribute meaningful solutions to designs. His practical experience in property development provides him with a distinctive perspective that bridges the gap between architectural vision and real-world project delivery, understanding both the creative and commercial aspects of building design.

Professional Philosophy

Gbolahan believes that great architecture emerges from strong collaboration and a deep understanding of the practical realities of building. His experience in property development has taught him that the best designs balance aesthetic vision with cost efficiency, client taste, and functional excellence. He approaches every project as a team effort, eager to learn from colleagues and contribute meaningful solutions.
