Olugboyega Tayo-Ojo

Architect committed to finding balance between innovation and historical context, with over 7 years of experience in architecture, construction, interior design, and landscape design.

With an average of 7 years working experience in the field of architecture, building construction, interior design, and landscape design, this Architect obtained fundamental working experience with Project Consultants and Development Architects (PCDA) in Abuja for 2 years, where he served as a Graduate Architect.

He joined The Building Practice Ltd. in 2015, in a pursuit for contemporary architectural practice, improved building construction knowledge, and quality service delivery. Since joining TBP, he has contributed significantly to numerous projects while maintaining a strong commitment to personal development and professional excellence.

He is committed to building a reputable career through a constant quest for improvement, personal development and paying attention to history of place while contributing his quota to jobs delivery and also contributing to a healthy work environment everywhere he is privileged to serve and work as an Architect.

Design Philosophy

"Seeking new ways to solving problems without erasing the impact of history is my way of 'finding balance' in life."

This philosophy guides his approach to every project. He believes that architecture should not only address contemporary needs and embrace innovation but also honor and preserve the historical and cultural context of each place. By finding this balance, he creates designs that are both forward-thinking and deeply rooted in their environment.

Approach to Architecture

Finding Balance

The core of his architectural philosophy lies in finding balance—between the old and the new, between preservation and innovation, between respecting history and embracing contemporary solutions. This approach ensures that his designs are contextually appropriate while meeting modern functional requirements.

History-Conscious Design

By paying attention to the history of place, he ensures that each project is grounded in its unique cultural, historical, and environmental context. This sensitivity to place creates architecture that resonates with its surroundings and contributes positively to the built environment.

Innovative Problem Solving

He seeks new ways to solve architectural problems, embracing innovation and contemporary methodologies while ensuring that these solutions don't erase or disrespect the impact of history. This balanced approach results in architecture that is both progressive and respectful.

Continuous Development

Committed to a constant quest for improvement and personal development, he actively seeks opportunities to expand his knowledge, refine his skills, and stay current with evolving architectural practices and technologies. This dedication to growth ensures that his work remains relevant and of the highest quality.
