Adegoke Adewunmi James is a seasoned Construction Project Manager with over a decade of hands-on experience delivering private, commercial, and industrial construction projects across Nigeria.

He possesses a strong track record of successfully managing projects from conception to completion, ensuring quality, efficiency, and stakeholder satisfaction.

A COREN-certified engineer and member of both the Nigerian Society of Engineers and the Project Management Institute, Adegoke combines technical expertise with strategic project leadership. He is recognized for driving project excellence through effective planning, resource optimization, cost control, quality management, and stakeholder engagement.

With a results-oriented approach and a passion for delivering sustainable development initiatives, he consistently leads multidisciplinary teams to achieve outstanding outcomes while contributing to organizational growth and community impact.

Professional Philosophy

James believes successful project delivery depends on technical discipline, strategic planning, resource optimization, and steady stakeholder alignment from conception to completion.
