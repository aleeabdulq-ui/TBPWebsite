Nicole Duke

Talented young architect and digital artist with over 3 years of experience, specializing in sustainable design and innovative architectural solutions.

Nicole is a graduate of Caleb University, holding both a Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Architecture. Asides from being a young architect, she is also a digital artist. Her art background helps her understand colour theory, the concept of shapes and form which gives her an advantage while designing. She is also good team player, a fast learner and a hard worker. Her interests include Landscape architecture, building facade design and biophilic architecture.

She has been with The Building Practice Ltd. team since 2021, bringing her expertise in architectural design, sustainable solutions, and innovative thinking to every project she undertakes.

Professional Philosophy

Nicole believes in creating sustainable and innovative architectural solutions that harmonize with nature and enhance human well-being. Her approach combines artistic creativity with technical expertise to deliver designs that are both aesthetically pleasing and environmentally responsible.
