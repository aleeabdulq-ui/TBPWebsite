Ikwuazom Somtochukwu Henry is the SEO and Digital Marketing Manager at The Building Practice Ltd, focused on turning online visibility into real, measurable business growth. His role bridges the gap between the firm's exceptional architectural work and the digital audience who needs to find it.

Henry helps The Building Practice improve search rankings, optimize ad spend, and attract high-quality customers using data-driven SEO, targeted Google Ads campaigns, and a full-funnel digital marketing approach. His goal isn't just traffic — it's meaningful engagement, conversions, and long-term growth for the firm.

By positioning The Building Practice prominently in search results and across digital channels, Henry ensures that the firm's portfolio, expertise, and services reach the right audience at the right time — converting online interest into real-world project enquiries and client relationships.
