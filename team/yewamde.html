Yewande Adeyemi (ACIPM) is an enthusiastic, passionate, result-driven Human Resource person and Administrative Officer at The Building Practice Ltd. She is an Associate Member of Chartered Institute of Personnel Management of Nigeria (CIPM), she's also a member of The Nigerian Institute of Management (NIM).

She completed her Higher National Diploma and National Diploma in Business Administration at Moshood Abiola Polytechnic, She has obtained quite a number of soft skill training and training in advanced human Resources Management and Human Resources Generalist.

She's highly proactive, open to learning, and enjoys being part of an energetic and dynamic team.
